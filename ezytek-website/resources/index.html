Frequently asked questions

What is Sage 300 ERP?

An ERP system covering finance, inventory, sales, purchasing and related operations for growing organizations.

Which industries can use Sage 300?

Manufacturing, FMCG, pharmaceuticals, trading and distribution, retail, engineering, import and export, and services.

Does EZYTEK provide new Sage 300 implementation?

Yes. Our six-step method runs from requirement analysis to post-go-live support.

Can EZYTEK support existing Sage 300 installations?

Yes, remotely and on-site, including troubleshooting, SQL maintenance and upgrade assistance.

Do you provide Annual Maintenance Contracts?

Yes. Essential, Professional and Enterprise packages, with terms confirmed in your agreement.

Can you customize Crystal Reports?

Yes. We build and modify financial, operational and statutory reports.

Can Sage 300 integrate with Power BI?

Yes. We design the data refresh method, licensing and security for each customer.

Can Sage 300 data be used with AI tools securely?

Yes, through a governed integration layer with role-based access, audit logs and human review.

Do you provide remote support outside Bangladesh?

Please contact us with your country and requirements so we can confirm what we can deliver.

Can data be migrated from another ERP?

Yes. Agreed records are prepared, migrated and validated during user acceptance testing.

Do you provide functional and technical training?

Yes, for finance, inventory, administration, SQL, Crystal Reports, Sage Intelligence and Power BI.

What affects the implementation timeline and cost?

Modules, users, locations, data migration scope, customizations, integrations and reporting needs.
